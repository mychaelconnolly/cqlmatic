- Field discovery now counts all tag candidates in one tag batch per source, of at most 20 columns, after the field listings and before kind facets and population. A Quick sources + fields run needs at least 5 searches per source instead of 13, and at most 22 instead of 30.
- Population reuses a tag batch count only for the same field over the same window; the remaining fields are grouped by their actual window, so a window of 15 minutes or less takes one population search where it took two. Tag columns that were not selected stay observations without a rate and take no population place.
- A selected field that needs a whole-window count keeps it even when the recent tag batch found the field absent, and a recent zero no longer hides presence that a whole-window count or dataset or kind facet saw in the same run, so strict `check --require-observed` grounds those fields.
