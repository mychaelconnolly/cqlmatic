- `prepare-discovery` now refuses, before it writes anything, a request whose `max_queries` is below the searches needed when every selected source has events (the reply names that minimum), a window that ends after the current time, an active pack created after the current time, and more than 100 sources. The offline plan refuses more than 100 sources too.
- Search figures now report `minimum_all_live` and `below_expected_high`. `expected_low` is the fewest searches of a run that activates with every other source quiet, and `expected_high` is the most any run can take, no longer capped by the limit; Full discovery now has an upper figure. With the standard candidates and default 20-field population cap, a Quick sources + fields source needs at least 4 searches and at most 21 (additional `kind_fields`, `value_fields`, extra tag batches or a different cap are reflected in the request-specific `expected_high`). Checkpoints of the earlier plan keep their own figures.
- A literal type selector supplies its `#type` facet from the source volume without a search, and in Full mode an `#event.dataset` facet requested in `kind_fields` comes from a dataset listing of fewer than 50 rows, ordered by count and then value.
