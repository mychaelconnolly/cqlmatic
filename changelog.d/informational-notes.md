- These diagnostics from `check` and `generate` are now informational instead of warnings: `CQL_GROUPBY_LIMIT` with a numeric or omitted limit, `CQL_TARGET_REQUIREMENT`, `CQL_JOIN_COST`, `CQL_JOIN_AFTER_AGGREGATE`, `CQL_APPROXIMATE_DISTINCT`, `CQL_CORRELATION_CAPABILITY`, `CQL_RAW_SCAN_COST`, `CQL_LOOKUP_COMPLETENESS`, `CQL_TIME_BUCKET_LIMIT`, `CQL_COLLECT_LIMIT` and `CQL_SPLIT_SHAPE`. Their messages and conditions are unchanged and they still appear in the output. `--fail-on-warning` no longer exits 1 for a query whose only findings are these, and the search guard's note no longer lists them. 0.1.10 reported all of them as warnings.
