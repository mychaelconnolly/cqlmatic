- `env inventory` accepts a `batch` request: 1 to 32 `review-source`, `bind-parser`, `exclude-parser`, `parser-not-applicable`, `import-parsers` and `reconcile` actions in one command and one writer lock, applied in order with the checks each gets on its own. Either every item is saved or none is. The reply lists an outcome for each item and whether the batch was saved; a batch that saves nothing exits 2 with that reply on stdout. Pending inventory work is checked once, against the result of the whole batch. Single actions are unchanged.
